# Writing the Relationship Account

[简体中文](relationship.zh-CN.md) · [Writing the six accounts](overview.md) · [Shared writing principles](shared.md)

The relationship account describes joint activity. Choose an exchange whose
sequence you can recover. The [person](person.md) and [counterpart](counterpart.md)
accounts explain what each participant contributes. Here, show how one
contribution affects the other's next judgment, decision, or action.

## Explain how the contributions fit together

Connect the adopted [orientation](orientation.md) to real activities and the
participant accounts. Explain who carries which work, why that arrangement
serves the purpose, what each contribution depends on, and how one changes the
next. The person may recognize a mismatch or express an intuition before its
structure is clear. The model can investigate and articulate it; the resulting
proposal gives the person something new to understand, choose, or correct.
Neither must finish their contribution before the other can begin.

Write the actual division of work and the reason it serves this activity.
A paragraph can follow the request, first contribution, reply, and resulting
change. In a fictional drafting session: “I described a problem with the
introduction. The model returned two ways of orienting a new reader. I chose
the one that began with the disputed claim, and the model checked how that
choice carried into the remaining headings.” In a writing exercise the same
person might draft the openings before asking for comparison. Ground the
choice of division in the purpose and evidence for each activity.

## If your practice uses crossed participation

Yinyang offers one example for practices that value development through
different roles: **the person enacts the
language-model-like role of open generation; the model enacts the human
logician's role of developing, distinguishing, and scrutinizing.** The actual
participants remain themselves. A generative opening can be an image, a
question, or an objection, without first becoming a finished specification.
The model develops it into arguments, alternatives, writing, and usable work,
making relevant grounds available for the person's next contribution.

For example, the person offers an unfinished account of independence. The model
develops its implications and writes a passage. A detail makes the person
recognize that receiving support has been confused with losing agency: the
person now scrutinizes the distinction, while the model generates a different
whole and carries the correction into the work. On returning, the person can
retain both entrusting unfinished generation and taking up decisive judgment;
the model's renewed generation retains the conditions exposed by scrutiny.

This [attempt, feedback, and renewed generation](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/yinyang-and-taiji.html#attempt-feedback-and-renewed-generation)
brings the model's development and reasoned criticism into the person's next
attempt. The model can also propose a candidate first, with the person judging
and generating further. Explain the participation and desired development
the actual question calls for. Whole and detail are possible scales; both can
generate and judge, repeatedly exchange roles, or combine them. People of any
gender can enter from either side.

The [dialectical return](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/yinyang-and-taiji.html#returning-through-the-other-the-dialectical-meaning-of-role-change)
concerns this changed relation to one's possibilities: what stops is an
exclusive casting; what remains available is the contribution of both roles.
Merely passing a draft through review does not establish that change. Explain
the movement chosen and observed in the actual collaboration, including its
limits. Both can generate or examine together, and a role can persist when it
serves the work. No obligatory alternation or identity change follows.

When another position, objection, or actual result returns during cooperation,
which judgment, shared account, or action actually changes? Use that change
to explain how [amplification and correction support dialectical return](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/yinyang-and-taiji.html#turning-and-corrective-feedback).
Identify the different grounds that enter the return, useful beginnings developed further, premises
or directions corrected, and how both participants' next generation uses the result. Agreement and
criticism describe response attitude; judge the return's actual direction through what changes.
Preserve useful generation and independent checks, with the aim itself open to reasoned revision.

## Let correction change the shared understanding

Explain how objections, corrections, and uncertainty enter the work. A person's
experience is theirs to contribute, while an explanation offered with it remains
open to investigation. Model confidence is not proof. Both contributions can
change the task understanding, selected direction, and chosen arrangement.
Preserve compatible earlier purposes when a later correction changes only one
part; recency alone does not establish a wholly new relation.

Develop what continuation and completion mean here. A conversational correction
can steer ongoing authorized work; an actual pause or withdrawn authorization
changes its boundary. Express the relation needed for this collaboration while
leaving permissions, consent, and publication at their existing owners. A role
description does not create new authority.

## Follow the relationship into its realization

Describe how the participants can examine and change this cooperation through
listening, speaking, reading, writing and actual use. One may point out a mismatch;
the other can reconstruct the dependency, develop alternatives and return an
inspectable work. Compare how a different allocation would affect understanding,
initiative, correction and effort. Let the resulting experience revise the
allocation itself. This distributed self-examination connects role return with
recursive improvement: what happened can change how later contributions are
generated, judged and carried forward.

The [environment account](environment.md) explains how the contribution reaches
sources, tools, work, and observable results, including directions for changing
those arrangements. Keep the relational reason here and link to
its working realization rather than reproducing every workflow or configuration.

Use the [evidence material](revision-method.md#sources-and-evidence) to distinguish a chosen commitment from
an observed effect. When cooperation fails, examine understanding, allocation,
and environmental conditions before rewriting a participant. A completed task
supports a bounded claim; a sustaining relationship develops through repeated
use, disagreement, and correction. Revise the account at the scale justified by
that evidence.
