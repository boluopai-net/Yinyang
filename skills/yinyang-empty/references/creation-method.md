**To use Yinyang for your own personal recursive self-improvement (RSI), you can skip the philosophical essays.** You can write your own views or ask a language model to read the [Core views](core-views.md), select a few you want to adopt, and add judgments of your own. Your essays and personal material can also yield views worth keeping in their own right.

Separately, have the language model distill your essays and personal material into a personal model expressed in six working files: orientation, person, counterpart, relationship, environment, and revision. The views and the personal model are independent. Where an account needs the reasoning behind an arrangement, it cites the relevant view and uses your own material to explain how it applies in your circumstances. Recurring methods in the material can also be developed into tools or Skills. As new material arrives, the language model can update the relevant views, accounts, and tools automatically or help you revise them.

Supply your selected views and six accounts in the model’s global prompt for everyday work. Have it configure and revise your software environment, including your own Skills, in light of them. Practice noticing a problem and explaining it in ordinary language, ask the model for a counterexample, and try your explanation in the situation itself. Let a mismatch change the relevant account or software setting and see what happens next. That negative feedback gives you a way to cultivate intuition, embodied reasoning, and everyday reasoning in language as you use the environment.

## Philosophy and the personal environment revise one another

Begin with something you are doing. Have the model adjust the environment using the six accounts, try the change, and identify which understanding needs revision. If the difficulty recurs, revise the method for noticing and resolving it. On the next occasion, check whether the new method finds the problem earlier and makes an effective change easier. This is where strong RSI enters the practice. [Writing one's own recursive improvement through literature](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/literary-recursive-improvement.html#from-rewriting-words-to-changing-the-next-activity) develops the mechanism; [its reading example](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/literary-recursive-improvement.html#how-one-reading-changes-the-way-reading-is-improved) shows how to test improvement in the method itself.

## Begin with Empty

The public Skill is **Empty**. The author's philosophical essays and diagrams remain available as reading material; your personal version develops its own adopted understanding.

**Empty provides the recursive self-improvement method.** Form personal content from your own purposes, experience, material, and judgment, then revise it through actual work.

The six accounts give this method a working form: direction, actual participants,
their cooperation, its realization, and the way it changes itself. Read them
together to understand an activity, explore a candidate change, compare its
effects, and carry the selected revision into use. The
[RSI essay](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/literary-recursive-improvement.html) develops the argument; the
[six writing guides](design-and-revision.md#form-a-situated-version-from-actual-material)
explain how to form the accounts from your own material.

[**Empty method source**](https://github.com/boluopai-net/Yinyang/tree/main/skills/yinyang-empty) is the entrance for your own practice.

## Choose assistance for the same method

### Direct model writing

Give the language model you currently use your own philosophical understanding, Personal material, and existing accounts.
Use the [six formation guides](design-and-revision.md#form-a-situated-version-from-actual-material)
to condense, organize, and revise them directly. As model capabilities develop, let newer models reinterpret
the material and improve its condensation. Keep the six files in private `references/situated/`,
with versions in private Git/GitHub.

### Optional memory and synthesis assistance

Add a community project for memory and working-account formation to your Empty-based personal version.
This project uses [Hindsight](hindsight.md): it retains sources, integrates observations, and forms
maintained accounts from questions. The model carries configuration, checking, and revision,
returning adopted results to the six accounts.
The [Hindsight configuration guide](hindsight.md#configure-the-selected-native-implementation)
is the operational entrance.

Choose assistance by the actual retrieval, integration and maintenance work it
improves. Both implementations retain the same six questions, attributable
grounds, whole-account checking and return from use. Tool choice can change
as the material, model and maintenance costs change.

## Where the six accounts live and how to use them

Orientation, person, counterpart, relationship, environment, and revision live in private
`references/situated/`; the [file and update contract](package.md#common-and-situated-content)
lists their filenames. Personal material, readable topic articles, and the source index live in
`references/personal/`. A native implementation can also bind the six complete bodies through a private index.

In an actual task, the model reads these accounts together to understand the person's direction, the participants and their relationship, the available environment, and how their arrangement can change. For example, when helping someone resume reading, it can recover the passage and the person's question, prepare a useful view, and use their feedback to revise that support.

For formation and revision, the model reads adopted philosophy and relevant fresh sources, corrects Personal
understanding, synthesizes and reads the six accounts together, then realizes selected changes in the working
environment and revises from use. [Forming and revising working models](design-and-revision.md)
develops the writing method; [everyday collaboration](https://github.com/boluopai-net/Yinyang/blob/main/skills/yinyang-empty/SKILL.md) explains reading and using the current accounts.

## Begin with the material you already have

Existing philosophy, personal experience, preferences, works, or a working environment can provide the starting point.
The model helps form missing connections; actual work can also change the philosophy and collaboration.
For further writing, [An example and a commons](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/example-and-commons.html) opens a path to personal essays and different works.
