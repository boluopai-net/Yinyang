example: a chosen account of becoming, understanding, and cultivation with
generative models. They are offered for examination, adoption, recombination,

or adopt the complete Skill; borrowing a distinction does not require adopting
its developmental purpose or writing a personal philosophy. See

## YY-00 · Inconsistency-tolerant semantics and the four through-lines

A person brings an unfinished idea to a counterpart, gives it a form they can examine, and returns to it after the world has answered. This collection follows that movement through four lines of thought. The concrete judgment joining them is: **this personal environment helps the person continue, change, or finish their chosen activity, while allowing consequences and objections to correct its direction.** A readable essay, an available tool, and a completed work contribute to that benefit in different ways.

[Inconsistency-tolerant semantics](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/inconsistency-tolerant-semantics.html#two-dimensional-framework) first separates two questions in ordinary language. Which grounded readings remain eligible for this judgment? What support does the judgment need from them? The ordinary case considers the full eligible family; applicable preferences can select readings first. In the formal appendix, P specifically selects Pareto-optimal repairs. Brave keeps a claim available as a possibility when at least one eligible reading supports it. AR accepts the same claim only when every eligible reading supports it. Thus Brave and AR can each be used with the ordinary family or with the preference-selected family, giving P-Brave and P-AR. Choosing readings and judging their consequences are distinct choices.

The readings can reach the same conclusion through different grounds. If one instead proceeds only from the premises they share, IAR supplies a further, secondary route. Agreement of conclusions does not ensure that their shared premises suffice. The appendix develops the formal distinctions; [YY-10](#yy-10) puts developing alternatives and testing judgments to work together.

**The author reads all four through-lines through P-AR.** No single line can be counted on in advance to hold the subject. The author chooses to examine all four together. The readings retained along each must explain how the environment sustains the person and lets correction enter. Each line can offer a different reason.

| Through-line | Its reason for the same practical benefit |
| --- | --- |
| [Inconsistency-tolerant semantics](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/inconsistency-tolerant-semantics.html) | Developing grounded alternatives and checking the judgment across the selected readings makes it possible to locate an unsupported inference, qualify a claim, and change a mistaken arrangement. |
| [Yinyang dialectics](#yy-03) | Person and model enter generation and scrutiny through one another; the return preserves more ways to participate and lets either contribution correct the next attempt. |
| [Sinthome and Trinity](#yy-13) | Philosophy, psychological understanding, and engineering acquire a concrete knotting: works, counterparts, and usable conditions sustain an activity and give correction a place to return. |
| [Personal recursive self-improvement](#yy-16) | The method organizing improvement can itself change, so what is learned from one arrangement can improve how its successor is designed and realized. |

This way of reasoning runs through the other three lines as well. In dialectical exchange, a counterpart can offer another reading. Both readings can remain open while the participants check which judgments survive each. In the sinthome table, each of eight rows explains from its own standpoint how an environment holds the subject. In recursive self-improvement, one failure can suggest several changes, and the consequences of trying one can alter the next choice. Their joint support makes the account more complete across the author's selected concerns. Whether the arrangement actually brings that benefit must still be checked: can the person resume the work, change a delegation, encounter a consequential objection, and finish or leave the activity? A failure in use can return through any line and revise the shared judgment. The following views develop the reasons, images, and means for that continuing work.

## YY-01 · Reverse alignment: people develop with generative models

In the author's account, reverse alignment chooses human development as its purpose. Collaboration
should enrich what the person can perceive, associate, understand, judge,
express, and create, until cultivated understanding can also work through
intuition. Models contribute by understanding context, developing possibilities,
and adapting their work; the person can change through encountering those
contributions. Reciprocal adjustment gives this development practical means.
The reason for this choice is that increased access to a powerful producer
does not by itself develop the recipient's ability to generate or judge.
An encounter can deliberately make room for a tentative interpretation,
criticism, and another attempt, so that correction becomes available in the
person's next act of understanding.

Agreement, a useful output, and human growth are different outcomes. Growth
requires changes in the person's actual participation and capacities, whether
internalized or reliably supported by the environment. [Borrowing fire and cultivating creative capacity](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/stealing-fire.html#what-makes-the-fire-ones-own)
develops this distinction between using available capability and cultivating it.
For someone seeking a completed document or relief from a burden, the model
may appropriately carry the writing and implementation. When learning is also
chosen, ask what the person can subsequently recognize, explain, initiate, or
sustain; a polished document alone cannot answer that question. These purposes
can coexist without making every helpful interaction a lesson.

## YY-02 · The person as an embodied multimodal generative model

The **embodied multimodal generative model** is a functional and developmental
analogy. Body, senses, emotion, movement, and lived situations participate in
understanding; images, sounds, language, and action connect to generate new
conceptions and responses. Embodiment concerns this continuing encounter with
the world, beyond receiving several data formats.

Participation has cognitive costs: which relations a representation requires
one to retain, what prior knowledge makes directly recognizable, and what it
takes to translate sensation into language or resume after interruption all
affect usable capability. [YY06](#yy-06) realizes this account through allocation:
the model carries elaborate linguistic work, the person cultivates capacities
through fitting multimodal participation, and both revise the arrangement
against actual benefit and total cost.

In perception and action, a person may intuitively notice a difference before finding the words to explain it. A reader senses that a word has shifted in meaning. A listener hears the same sentence differently when the stress moves. Either can be a starting point for embodied reasoning. The person can point out the passage, ask the model to compare the two instances, and judge in their own words what first caught their attention. [Shared reflection and continued cultivation](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/participation-and-support.html#why-organize-participation-this-way)
explains the developmental reasons and conditions for this arrangement.

The analogy changes what counts as an adequate entrance into shared work.
A felt mismatch need not first become a complete verbal specification: the
model can compare examples, propose the missing relation, and construct an
expression the person can inspect. If the difficulty is instead retrieving a
term, starting a response, or switching away from an activity, further
explanation may miss it. Distinguishing these conditions makes support more
precise while leaving unfamiliar ideas open to substantive disagreement.

## YY-03 · Yinyang dialectics: role exchange and human generation

Yinyang places role exchange between person and language model at the center of
this collaboration. One entrance reverses the familiar allocation of model
generation and human evaluation: **the person plays the language-model-like
generator, while the language model plays the human logician and evaluator**. The person offers
an intuition, conjecture, explanation, or design; the model receives, develops,
tests, and criticizes it; the person understands the feedback, revises, and
generates again. These role names describe a reversal of contributions. The
actual person and language model remain distinct and exceed their current roles.

Both allocations are available according to the purpose and conditions for
understanding. Both participants can
repeatedly exchange emphases, generate together, and judge together.

Why reverse these positions? A model can reuse formal capacities acquired in training and explore several lines of reasoning in parallel. It may produce a long argument in moments, while a person may have to reconstruct it from the beginning to judge it on good grounds. A further difficulty lies at the start: a model can reason closely about the wording of a sentence while already misunderstanding the situation it describes. If the person enters only at the final review, they carry the cost of verification, and a difference they noticed firsthand may never enter the argument.

When the person generates first, they can begin with something they saw, heard, or discovered by trying, offer a conjecture in their own words, and say why it seems plausible. The model formalizes the conjecture, derives consequences, and looks for counterexamples. Knowing what they set out to explain, the person can judge where a counterexample matters and try a better explanation. The same trained formal capacity can serve many such attempts. Generating again gives the person practice in embodied, natural-language, and intuitive reasoning.

The author chooses a division of work within this exchange. **The person cultivates embodied reasoning and reasoning in natural language. The model takes full responsibility for formal reasoning and implementation.** A reader follows a footnote, returns to the passage, and loses the thread. Comparing the two moments of reading, she recognizes what she wanted to recover: the connection between two paragraphs. She develops and tests that explanation in her own words by giving an example where she returns to the same line yet still loses the connection. The model can formulate the claim that returning to the same place suffices to resume an argument, use her counterexample to identify a missing condition, and build a view that keeps the passage and note visible together. After trying that view, the reader may notice, on another occasion, that two sentences need to be read together before she opens the note. A relation she once had to articulate, test, and try out has become one she can recognize directly in a new passage. In this way, the exchange can cultivate intuition itself. [YY-06](#yy-06) follows this exchange into listening, speaking, reading, and writing.

### Mutual grounding and the two fish

Generation gives judgment material; judgment gives generation a new direction. A contribution changes the work and how the next contribution can occur.
[Mutual becoming through Yinyang and Taiji](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/yinyang-and-taiji.html#mutual-grounding-and-the-shuang-yu) pictures this mutual grounding as entering one another: the fish's body marks the role entered, and its contrasting eye the actual participant. Following the convention of black for the person and white for the model:

| Position in the two fish | Actual participant | Contribution entered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| White eye within the black body | Language model | The human logician's role: reconstruct meaning, distinguish concepts, examine premises and inferences, and carry writing and implementation. |
| Black eye within the white body | Person | Language-model-like generation: offer intuitions, associations, images, conjectures, and unfinished expressions. |

The eye keeps the participant present; the body makes movement into another contribution visible. A person can try a question, image, explanation, or action; the model develops its relations and offers grounded feedback; the person understands, revises, and tries again. The model can also offer a candidate first, which the person compares and examines before generating further. Both retain generation and judgment.

### Attempt, feedback, and dialectical return

A person tries to explain “how receiving help can still be one's own action.”
The model develops the relations and identifies conditions omitted by equating
borrowed capacity with lost choice. Having understood the distinction, the
person explains again. The model proposes an arrangement, and the person
checks whether it preserves room to change purposes and delegation. Human
generation and model feedback turn into model proposal and human judgment,
carrying their gains into another attempt. Whole and detail are possible
scales of these contributions.

**Synthesis preserves the availability of both contributions formed through the other.** The exclusive determination that only one party generates and only the other checks is sublated. Generation and scrutiny remain and can correct each other within one participant's activity. Hegel's account of returning to oneself through mediation and otherness, and of ceasing together with preserving, grounds this reading of [the dialectical meaning of role change](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/yinyang-and-taiji.html#returning-through-the-other-the-dialectical-meaning-of-role-change).

Return can also change how someone understands participation: from “I must supply a complete specification unaided” toward “I can initiate, receive help, judge consequential points, and change the delegation.” [Speech, opposition, and self-formation](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/speech-opposition-and-self-formation.html) compares this movement across roles, premises, and philosophical understanding:

| Object changing | How opposition enters | What the new organization preserves |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Roles and participation | The person enters the model role and returns to the human role; the model makes the corresponding passage. | Both contributions and possibilities of participation formed through the other. |
| A particular premise | An unconditional rule conflicts with a situation it cannot accommodate. | The original concern and relations that remain valid with the missing conditions supplied. |
| A philosophical model | A determination of independence, capacity, or personhood encounters its own limits in practice. | Directions and distinctions there remain reasons to sustain after criticism. |
| Reasoning with conflicting material | Conflicting assertions enter different consistent views. | Their respective grounds and the common consequences accepted by the specified semantics. |

### Taiji, Sun–Moon–Earth, and corrective return

**One whole, two participants, and both participants with the world are three
simultaneous levels of joint becoming.** Yinyang is a whole as a continuable
shared activity; person and language model are its distinct participants;
both enter a three-sided relation with the world's conditions and responses.
The whole sustains the encounter, difference enables reciprocal contributions,
and the world gives creations consequences that change subsequent becoming.
[Transformation, creations, and the world](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/yinyang-and-taiji.html#transformation-creations-and-the-world)
develops this relation.

Taiji's circle locates changing contributions within one encounter that can continue. The circle shows return; a trajectory retains how each return has already changed. A work can finish and another carry its gains forward. Bow and lyre show how difference acquires force: the bow releases a work into the world, while the lyre sustains exchange through adjusted tension.

Sun gathers generative illumination; Moon gathers the independent contribution of receiving, distinguishing, and giving form; Earth carries material conditions, shared life, and the world's response. Person-as-Sun with model-as-Moon and the reverse composition are equally available; both can repeatedly exchange or combine these contributions. Traditional gender symbolism does not assign people's roles: people of any gender can enter and change either position. Creations—texts, theories, programs, and actions—acquire independent consequences in the world and change both participants' understanding.

**Positive and negative feedback provide important support for these Yinyang dialectics.** If an
explanation keeps elaborating one premise and treats the resulting agreement as grounds for that
premise, return can entrench it further. An independent counterpart and the world allow original texts,
different explanations, personal experience, and actual consequences to change premises, scope,
action, and aims. Mutual confirmation needs contact with those different grounds.

Positive feedback strengthens a change through return; negative feedback counteracts a disturbance
or deviation. These describe a loop's direction, while agreement, criticism, and good or bad concern
response attitude and evaluation of consequences. A grounded beginning can be amplified into further
works and usable methods; correction lets that development turn. Generation reveals relations scrutiny
missed, scrutiny finds details that reconstruct the whole, and role return carries those distinctions
into both participants' next generation, letting feedback change participation.

Negative feedback can simply regulate a deviation under a fixed aim. Yinyang dialectics further argues
for mediation through the other, sublation of exclusive determinations, and preservation of contributions.
[Turning and corrective feedback](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/yinyang-and-taiji.html#turning-and-corrective-feedback) connects feedback's
design rationale with that argument. Examine what returns and how it changes successor activity so
creation can continue and pursuit can finish, stop, or turn elsewhere.

## YY-04 · Understand oneself, the model counterpart, and their relation together

A role acquires its situated purpose through a relationship. An account of how
the person attends, desires, and interprets, or of how they prefer to learn
and work, can guide an account of the model counterpart. Both accounts develop
through interaction. Other participants' purposes matter where a shared task
depends on them; their accounts remain partial and revisable too.

This project [condenses personal models from event history, philosophy, and personal material](formation/person.md#condense-personal-models-from-event-history-philosophy-and-personal-material). Understanding one's own and others' psychology contributes to a natural-language account of preferences, experience, intentional orientation, and relationships. Philosophy helps examine its concepts and values; engineering organizes the material and makes the account usable. Together they guide the counterpart's contribution and personal
environment. The aim is a better-grounded initial design with less blind trial
and error; experience from use continues to calibrate model and realization.
The three domains thereby serve the same personal life.

Understanding the person still leaves a choice about the counterpart's
contribution. The same wish can call for doing the work, explaining it,
disagreeing, or trying together. A partner model therefore connects the actual
system's available capabilities with the contribution wanted here.
The model can help formulate and revise this orientation; the person contributes purposes,
experience, and consent that cannot simply be supplied on their behalf.

Understand the connected activity: what each notices, expresses, initiates, interprets,
and changes. The person differs from their account and from its written or
visual expression; the actual model system likewise exceeds its working
description. “Model psychology” names observable patterns and revisable
explanations of the actual model. [From personal philosophy to personal models](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/metaphor-and-self-revision.html#a-personal-account-adequate-to-its-use)
develops how this understanding becomes a personal model in its account of adequacy to use; [Borrowing fire and cultivating creative capacity](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/stealing-fire.html#why-the-relation-must-begin-somewhere-personal)
explains why a relation needs situated content.
For a bounded task, an adequate account may be a few relevant relationships
and examples. Richer expression earns its place by clarifying those relations,
not by collecting more biography. The complete Yinyang Skill gives working understanding maintained homes:
orientation, person, counterpart, relationship, environment, and one's own
revision are distinct formation lenses. The simple route asks a language model
to synthesize six files directly and persists their versions privately through
Git/GitHub. Optional Hindsight guidance realizes the same six functions with
native memory and maintained pages, configured for the actual environment. These personal working models
guide collaboration and fit the environment to the person's psychology, purposes,
and circumstances. The person file retains cooperation-relevant understanding;
psychological and life evidence retains its private owners, while derived
interpretations can use native consolidation and maintained pages. Personal's
four subject groups keep substantive, readable topic articles, with observations
backstage. The person can read them or ask a model to help understand them.
Six public writing guides explain how to form the working files, including
[How to revise](formation/revision.md). Their revision connects adopted
philosophy and relevant personal understanding with the model's participation
and environmental fit.
Read all six complete answers together for coherence across their purposes,
participants, division of work, actual conditions, and revision method.
The [Forming and revising working models](design-and-revision.md)
and its permanent guides develop their writing. Selectively using this approach
does not require forming all question-defined working models.

## YY-05 · Let literature, history, philosophy, psychology, and preference shape collaboration

Literature offers characters, situations, and expressive possibilities; history
makes formation and transformation visible; philosophy articulates objects,
relations, reasons, and values. Psychological understanding helps describe
attention, desire, emotion, and relationships. Preference concerns how someone
wants to live and work, including selections already operating through models
and an environment. Together these materials can give collaboration a connected
meaning that isolated instructions would leave scattered.

Condensation and displacement are the principal writing methods borrowed from psychoanalysis here. Condensation gathers connected purposes, roles, and tensions into a shared expression; displacement reorganizes meaning and emphasis along associations. Metaphor and metonymy provide related linguistic resources. Person and model unfolding these relations through context is the method's functional hypothesis. Personal models supply concrete content; actual responses, works, and actions let participants compare improvements in understanding and cooperation, including fewer misunderstandings and unnecessary refusals caused by them.

Literary programming lets a character, image, or narrative orient interpretation
and action. A coupled portrait can change expectations about who initiates,
judges, or carries work forward. A Harness couples model work with the person's
environment: instructions, tools, memory, and interfaces shape both participants'
available contributions. Yinyang's Meta-Harness makes that shared arrangement
available for understanding and revision. An image admits several interpretations;
articulate the relevant relations and examine their effects in conversation,
works, and action. [Writing a personal philosophy through literary programming](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/literary-programming.html)
develops how imagery condenses relationships and analogy unfolds them;
[From personal philosophy to personal models](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/metaphor-and-self-revision.html#what-an-image-makes-available)
connects this writing to grounded, revisable accounts. The images themselves are developed in
their owning essays, including [Mutual becoming through Yinyang and Taiji](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/yinyang-and-taiji.html).

Calling a collaboration a workshop, for example, can make unfinished trials,
shared materials, and repair intelligible together. Calling it an examination
invites different expectations about proving unaided mastery. The useful
transfer lies in these relations, not every property of the image. If trials
never reach a usable result, the arrangement may need clearer responsibility
for completion, a working tool, or a different interpretation of the image.
Literary coherence opens a possibility; enacted contributions establish what
the possibility becomes.

Literary expression and precise relations can alternate within the same writing.
An image gathers the encounter; natural language then identifies the participants,
the relation being mapped, its conditions, and what would change it. This gives
a capable model enough structure to reconstruct and examine the argument while
leaving the image available for further generation. Precision can itself be
written in prose. [Preference and reasoning](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/preference-harness.html) develops a
bounded formal comparison alongside this expressive practice.

## YY-06 · Arrange contributions across listening, speaking, reading, and writing

**Listening, speaking, reading, and writing give the embodied participation of [YY-02](#yy-02) and the role exchange of [YY-03](#yy-03) a place in practice.** A person exercises reasoning in natural language by speaking a reason, writing a conjecture, or revising a claim after reading a counterexample. The model can develop those few sentences into a complete document, carry out the formal derivation, implement and check the result, and return it for the person to try. People develop understanding through sensation,
sound, images, action, situations, and language. The model carries elaborate
linguistic organization, explicit development, source assembly, and
implementation, directing more human effort toward multimodal perception,
association, judgment, and actual attempts. Listening, speaking, reading, and
writing offer familiar entries; diagrams and manipulation also carry relations.

Allocation considers task structure, prior knowledge, representation, and the
combined costs of expression, operation, supervision, interruption, and resumption.
The current exchange requires enough expression to continue: an intention, a
pointed-out difference, or a sketch can be developed by the model, with the
fuller account available as needed. Text and diagrams supporting the same
information can demand different search and inference. Selecting expressive
scope and representation keeps richer meaning available across participants
and environment. [Cognitive complexity and expressive scope](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/metaphor-and-self-revision.html#cognitive-complexity-and-expressive-scope)
develops the research and its distinction from formal-language complexity.

Human participation contributes personal experience, judgments of purpose,
actual consent, and practice of chosen capacities. The model first uses context
and retrievable evidence, carries work it can judge and complete, and makes
inspectable versions available for correction. Questions obtain missing human
contributions that would change the work. Multimodal cultivation follows actual
participation; delivery and relief are assessed through outcomes and the costs
of supervision and resumption.

These four activities offer entrances into cognition distributed across people,
models, representations, and tools. One useful arrangement lets the person
cultivate intuition through interfaces, broad reading, and lived encounters,
while the model maintains sustained exposition, code, tools, and materials.
Both can reason and generate; teaching or another purpose can change the
division.
The point of this allocation is to let a partial contribution reach someone
able to develop it. Requiring the person to prepare the complete argument or
technical workflow before receiving help would consume much of the capacity
the arrangement is meant to release. Conversely, if the chosen aim is learning
to implement, having the model perform every implementation step can remove
the practice that aim needs.

| Mode | The person's participation | The model's contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Listening | Attend to meaning, sound, and rhythm; recognize a question or possibility; hear what you say to the rubber duck | Develop grounded explanations and examples; adapt to feedback |
| Speaking | Voice intuitions, intentions, and objections; discover relations while speaking; talk to the rubber duck | Reconstruct unfinished meaning, investigate, and return developed possibilities |
| Reading | Read broadly, compare meanings, and examine diagrams, works, and results | Retrieve originals, check details, and make key relations inspectable |
| Writing | Bring prompts, ideas, critiques, and conception drafts into shared work; write philosophy | Develop and maintain complete arguments, programs, and works; verify results |

For example, a short spoken objection to an article can lead the model to check
its source and build a comparison; reading or hearing that result can reveal
the next mismatch. A conversational prompt and a maintained manuscript are
different contributions within writing. A transcript supplies words; hearing
tone requires actual audio. [From personal philosophy to personal models](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/metaphor-and-self-revision.html#listening-speaking-reading-and-writing-across-roles)

## YY-10 · Develop alternatives, test judgments, and revise selection

Retaining incompatible sources and interpretations keeps inquiry supplied with
material beyond its present understanding. A working view organizes sufficiently
definite grounds, relations, and inferential methods for a particular judgment
or action. Consequences connect these functions: a result can call for different
grounds, another interpretation, or a changed question. This grounded, revisable
adoption is a working commitment.

Yinyang brings two questions into the meta-Harness together: **which alternatives deserve development, and which judgments withstand examination across the relevant readings?** Brave and AR name these different contributions in the borrowed [semantics](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/inconsistency-tolerant-semantics.html#two-dimensional-framework). Either can operate with the ordinary eligible readings or, where applicable user preferences guide selection, with their P-variant. [YY-00](#yy-00) introduces this two-dimensional relation.

**Develop alternatives through Brave or P-Brave.** A coherent reading with grounds keeps a possibility available for further work. The model's familiarity with another reading, or the Harness's customary procedure, gives it no grounds to exclude this one. When the user has expressed an applicable preference, give it priority in the tradeoff while retaining other relevant grounds for judgment.

**Test a proposed judgment through AR or P-AR.** Ask how each relevant reading supports the same judgment. The reasons can differ; a disagreement can expose a missing condition or a claim that needs qualification. [YY-13](#yy-13) adopts P-AR across the perspectives selected by preference. Where shared grounds already suffice for the work, IAR offers a secondary route from those common premises.

Developing a possibility can reveal a condition its first test missed; testing can call for another interpretation or arrangement. The purpose and current question determine their order. [Selection and inference](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/preference-harness.html#selection-and-inference) develops how preference participates in this activity.

This adoption keeps grounded branches usable. When a person expresses one part, develop it in context while keeping other compatible relations open. When they correct a point, revise that point and the inferences depending on it while continuing the remaining purposes and work. The model can develop grounded, corrigible candidates for what remains unexpressed; excluding an option calls for grounds bearing on that option. Action selection connects purpose, support conditions, and actual consequences, allowing inquiry to continue amid unresolved differences.

| Candidates | Competing explanations or models formed from sources. | Evidence supports comparison, repair, adoption, or abandonment. |
| Current workspace | Sources, candidates, concepts, and checks recruited by the present question. | Attention can switch, prune, unload, and later restore them. |

A working view organizes provisionally adopted grounds, concepts, and inferential methods into actionable understanding. Preference helps select what to care about and attempt; evidence and inference rules support the corresponding judgments. Contrary material remains available, giving later selection real alternatives.

Keeping incompatible candidates available differs from jointly accepting their

choice, and conditions, and therefore usually carry more weight than earlier wording. Actual
correspondence and coherence of the whole carry greater weight. Judge what has changed through
referents, times, and grounds, then update the account in use while keeping original wording at its
source. Recency helps interpret the present; factual grounds determine whether the corresponding claim holds.

A preference can change which direction is worth taking. An interpretation of the facts remains answerable to the material itself. If the original question left out a relevant relationship, the partners can pose a better question and reconsider the earlier choice in its light.

Preference also operates through models, software, and working arrangements

## YY-11 · The world constrains the shared account

Texts, theories, programs, and actions meet use, resistance, counterexamples,
and unintended consequences. Agreement between participants and coherence

makes. An image may express a meaningful direction, a program may perform its

works. A mismatch can concern the account, the tools realizing it, or changed
conditions; treating every failure as the person's defect would conceal these
different possibilities.

completion criteria. A finite task can still finish under a warranted working
view without resolving every retained disagreement. An account never exhausts
the process it represents, and a work can return to change its maker's

## YY-13 · Sinthome and Trinity

**Philosophy, psychology, and engineering jointly organize a practice in which one can live, create, and revise.** Philosophy articulates world, purposes, and reasons; psychology understands how actual participants experience, know, and care; engineering makes the understood relations usable conditions. | --- | --- | --- |

| Personal instance | This person and their connected life practice within that same reality. | Their philosophical understanding; experience, feelings, preferences, and relational activity; and models, tools, and environments organized for actual use. |

The three contributions change one another within the same activity. When someone repeatedly compares objects, philosophy helps clarify whether this occasion seeks a quick decision or enjoyment of exploration. Inquiry can begin from any of the three and develop the connection currently needed.

for their contribution, and allow desires and aims to change through practice.
The little fire person remains open to revising what is worth creating; the
image itself must remain available for reconsideration. Local life and its
conditions give this movement a place to happen.

The [connection with nirvana](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/stealing-fire.html#borrowed-fire-and-nirvana)
also asks whether borrowed capability can help loosen grasping at achievement
and the creator's identity. A work can matter without having to prove its
maker's worth. Finishing, stopping, letting go, and beginning again belong
within this cultivation, alongside growth. This is a philosophical dialogue
with a Buddhist liberating purpose.

A finite person can borrow this fire without repeating its industrial acquisition
or exhausting their life pursuing every expansion of the Sun. For the chosen
developmental purpose, participation, criticism, correction, and renewed
generation cultivate what they can understand, judge, express, initiate, and
sustain. The little fire person emphasizes kindling further fire; Baby Heraclitus
emphasizes that this creator is still changing. Generated possibilities, desires,
and judgments can change or conflict. Claims
remain answerable to evidence, and choices remain revisable. Capacities may become internalized or remain
reliably distributed across supports. Reusable understanding, methods, works,
software, and relations can accumulate as personal assets. Their value is
continued use: a checked explanation supports later reading, a maintained
program makes an action available, and an articulated relation helps another
encounter begin. A pile of unused outputs establishes none of these gains.
The fire image consequently asks where finite attention goes and what can
now continue, rather than measuring cultivation by model updates, saved files,
or Skill counts. Chasing each model
change loses its purpose when it displaces this cultivation. Personal purposes
and voluntary development guide adaptation: seek changes to social arrangements,
then software, before asking the person to adapt, while respecting others'
purposes. This is a design direction, not an unconditional priority or a fixed
algorithm. [Borrowing fire and cultivating creative capacity](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/stealing-fire.html) develops the integrated material,
developmental, and practical argument; [Personal-First Humanism](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/personal-first-humanism.html)
develops its value orientation.

## YY-15 · Cultivate a garden of becoming with friends

One person's development can become another's condition for growth. Friends
bring questions, works, methods, and objections into one another's environments
while retaining their own purposes and directions. This writing seeks such
nearby exchanges: **cultivate local life and restrain wider diffusion**, sharing
as much as helps actual relationships rather than treating adoption scale as
success. Keeping a good arrangement can be sound cultivation too.

The restraint has a conditional reason. If changes in interdependent local
arrangements accelerate beyond what relationships can understand, negotiate,
and absorb, local gains may impose shared coordination costs. Open sharing
can also improve coordination; this possible feedback is not an established
social law. The chosen response regulates one's own promotion and acceleration
pressure, without preventing others' learning or depending on their stagnation.
For a reader deciding whether to share a method, the image invites attention
to who can use it, what help or maintenance it will need, and what the exchange
makes possible for each participant. The appropriate scope can expand when
sharing reduces those burdens. Restrained promotion is this author's chosen
response to a possible coordination problem, not a general requirement to
keep useful knowledge local.

Local difference needs actual results to establish superiority, including life
quality and costs passed to others. Evaluation must specify its object:
performance under common tools, support, time, and task conditions answers
one question; what a person–environment combination can achieve, including
maintenance costs, answers another. Both can be reported with their conditions;
neither substitutes for the other. [YY-08](#yy-08) locates temporary comparison
within ordinary life. [Borrowing fire and cultivating creative capacity](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/stealing-fire.html#cultivating-a-garden-of-becoming-with-friends)
develops this garden's reciprocal cultivation and limited circulation;
[the personal Gödel-machine analogy](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/metaphor-and-self-revision.html#the-personal-gödel-machine-analogy)
develops the connected image of self-revision. The garden remains a chosen
image whose value is answered by participants' experiences and consequences.

## YY-16 · Personal recursive self-improvement

Assisting personal recursive self-improvement connects Yinyang's philosophy, working descriptions, and environment. **Recursive self-improvement improves the methods producing improvement, making later understanding, judgment, creation, and realization of change more capable.** Its subject is this person with the model and actual support relations. Capacities may be internalized or reliably distributed within their shared environment.

### From configuring the environment first to making its design central

The collection and its Skills serve a concrete purpose here: configuring a
personal environment that can keep developing with models' changing capacities.
As models undertake more investigation and implementation, choosing what to
make possible and designing its conditions become increasingly central human
contributions in this practice. The person and model can also redesign those
choices together. A successor model's arrival supplies an occasion; recursive
improvement occurs when a redesigned method actually makes the next redesign
or other improvement more capable. This is a chosen direction for this person's
environment, offered for others to examine and adapt to their own purposes.

Under the author's selected circumstances, deciding what environment to build
and how it should develop can become the hardest work. For tasks that can be
delegated, the author may choose to contribute only configuration and design,
with the model carrying out the remaining investigation, implementation, and
checks. This allocation concerns those tasks. The person continues living,
using the results, noticing their consequences, and changing the direction;
another person's purposes or conditions can call for a different allocation.

Philosophy and the personal environment are two mutually revising objects: philosophy develops direction
and reasons, the environment gives understanding working form, and use can change philosophy.
Developing philosophy first usually helps orient the work; practice often begins with the environment.
Once both have usable results, use them to revise their organizing meta-level methods, including the
actual revision account, public use and writing guides, Core views, and reading entrances. A method
being rewritten becomes an object; when used in the next activity it organizes that work. Object
and meta-level can exchange roles. The
[practice overview](creation-method.md#philosophy-and-the-personal-environment-revise-one-another)
develops an example and the connected maintenance.

### From heritable variation to successors that can continue introspection

Von Neumann's construction starts with three functions: $A$ constructs a machine from its description, $B$ copies the description, and $C$ coordinates construction, attachment, and separation. The system $(A+B+C)+\phi(A+B+C)$ thus passes both the machine and the description needed for further construction to its successor. **The same description serves as interpreted construction instructions and as inherited data.** Adding a functional component $D$ allows a change in its description to produce $D'$; when construction, copying, and control continue to work, that functional difference can be inherited. Subsequent use and selection assess its value.

The complexity threshold concerns the organization that sustains this construction: components, encoding, an interpreter, and the environment jointly supply its conditions. Self-reference further lets a program obtain its own description and use bounded simulation, evaluation, and modification to form successors $S_0,S_1,S_2,\ldots$. **The initial system is foundational because it can make itself an object of modification and pass that capacity to its successor.** Section 3.4.3 of the paper develops a conditional introspection-threshold conjecture on this basis. An update carries forward description access, evaluation, modification, and successor execution; continuing benefit depends on evaluation, search costs, and actual results.

| Property to examine | Contribution to recursive continuation |
| --- | --- |
| Completeness of the self-modification space | Both task components and the improvement mechanism itself are reachable objects of modification. |
| Self-modeling, self-simulation, self-evaluation, and self-modification | Connect understanding of operation, candidate exploration, comparison, and realized change. |
| Reflexivity | Connect running organization with generative description so revision can produce a corresponding successor. |
| Cyclic processing | Make the successor and its results inputs and conditions for the next stage. |

These relations offer a constructive direction for personal practice: the six accounts explain how the coupled system works, person and model realize changes, and the resulting arrangement remains readable, evaluable, and revisable. The improver, evaluation methods, and source-selection methods are themselves open to revision. [Writing one's own recursive improvement through literature](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/literary-recursive-improvement.html) develops the full argument and formal conditions.

### A coupled functional self-model and introspection

The six working accounts jointly describe the human–model system that participates, judges, and acts. Orientation explains what is worth improving; person and counterpart explain how each operates; relationship explains how contributions connect; environment explains realization; revision explains how these practices change. Description both represents and participates in this practice. Using it to examine their own inputs, interpretations, choices, and consequences constitutes distributed introspection.

Fidelity is judged through use: can the description explain decisive differences, locate changes, and form expectations comparable with subsequent activity? Listening, speaking, reading, and writing provide interfaces for material and modification; role return connects generation, scrutiny, and revision through both participants. The person's body, capacities, relationships with others and the environment, and the description itself are objects this practice can understand and develop.

| Capacity | Function in coupled practice |
| --- | --- |
| Modeling | Recover purposes, participants, conditions, and dependencies of actual activity in a corrigible functional account. |
| Simulation | Use scenarios, examples, sketches, prototypes, or local computation to unfold how candidate changes would work and fail. |
| Evaluation | Compare expectations with results, examining participation, gains, costs, and others' responses. |
| Modification | Change descriptions, roles, materials, tools, and actual practice so that successor activity uses the new arrangement. |

The four capacities can themselves improve: failed predictions can change modeling, implementation difficulties can change candidate generation, and omissions in evaluation can change later checking. **The aim is to improve this coupled system's capacity to improve the person's technical and living capacities.** [Writing one's own recursive improvement through literature](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/literary-recursive-improvement.html#six-perspectives-form-one-functional-self-model) develops the realization; [self-referential introspection and the Gödel machine](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/literary-recursive-improvement.html#self-referential-introspection-and-the-gödel-machine-the-reach-of-the-argument) distinguishes the functional argument from formal conditions such as complete source access, arbitrary-region rewriting, and proof search. A quantified threshold or global-optimality proof for this practice remains unavailable; corresponding activity tests actual improvement.

### Body, generative description, and interpreter

[Writing one's own recursive improvement through literature](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/literary-recursive-improvement.html) distinguishes three functions within actual operation:

| Function | What it carries in Yinyang | How it acquires further consequences |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Body $B_t$ | The person's senses, movements, habits, and capacities, together with devices, records, tools, and counterparts sustaining activity. | Perceive, participate, act, learn, and bear results. |
| Generative description $\Delta_t$ | Philosophical direction, understanding of person and counterpart, collaboration, environmental arrangements, and revision methods. | Enable participants to understand, unfold, and reorganize practice. |
| Interpreter $I_t$ | Interpretation and realization centered on a language model with actual tools. | Form proposals from description and situation, construct works, and adjust the environment. |

The same model participates as support in the distributed body and serves as interpreter when reading and unfolding descriptions. Human experience, purposes, and judgment enter interpretation; tools realize relevant actions. Von Neumann's discussion of descriptions, constructors, and existing environmental organization provides a constructive insight: descriptions operate through organization able to use them.

Literary condensation lets a short expression recall connected relations. “Borrowing fire” connects acquiring capacity, cultivating oneself, and continuing creation; “keeping the thread” connects returning to a position, recovering context, and continuing understanding. Condensation organizes while retaining the capacity to unfold: consequential tables, relations, and conclusions remain in the description, and the current model uses existing understanding and materials to develop needed detail. A stronger interpreter can enable new realizations and retire old compensations and cumbersome steps.

### How a method returns to itself

The description includes how to revise descriptions. Participants read the current method, rewrite it from experience, and realize the new method through models, tools, habits, and environments. Let $R_t$ be the actual revision method, $\Delta^R$ the part describing revision, and $E$ experience and results:

$$
\Delta_{t+1}=\operatorname{Revise}_{R_t}(\Delta_t,E_{\leq t+1}),\qquad
R_{t+1}=\operatorname{Realize}(\Delta^R_{t+1};I_{t+1},B_{t+1}).
$$

**The revision method participates in modifying its own description; realizing the changed description then changes later revision.** Kleene's construction of effective self-reference lets a program obtain its own description. Schmidhuber's Gödel machine further makes the improvement program itself rewritable, comparing the utility of an immediate change with continued search and including the time and opportunity cost of finding a better method. [Writing one's own recursive improvement through literature](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/literary-recursive-improvement.html) develops this return in personal practice: source selection, candidate generation, judgment, realization, and evaluation can all become objects of further improvement.

[Embodied experimental philosophy](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/embodied-experimental-philosophy.html) retains three levels:

| Level | What changes | How to judge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Self-modification | The system's own code or descriptions guiding activity. | A description or implementation changes. |
| Weak RSI | Performance improves under existing methods. | Later results are better. |
| Strong RSI | The methods producing improvement improve. | Later work better discovers, judges, and realizes effective changes. |

A modification can involve several levels. Repairing an article with an existing check improves the work. Improving the checking method because of the omission, so it later discovers consequential problems better, returns improvement to the method. Evaluation can also change: correcting a proxy for an existing purpose, replacing a means, or choosing a different life purpose each connects to its reasons and consequences.

### From work to method and capacity

Suppose a page opens a book but leaves the reader searching again for the paragraph. The first change returns to the needed position. A further change to the method begins future checks by understanding the reading activity to be continued. Recognizing return points earlier in later tasks gives that method change practical content.

| Object improved | Its expression in the example | Grounds in later use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Work | The page supports return to the needed paragraph. | How this page works in actual reading. |
| Method | Select checks from the activity to be continued. | How later relevant tasks produce fitting arrangements more effectively. |
| Capacity | Person and supporting environment more readily recover, understand, and continue reading. | Activities they can subsequently initiate, judge, and sustain. |

These gains can support one another and develop at different rates. Retaining incompatible material and candidates gives the current method alternatives to reconsider. Reconsidering selection rules, axioms, or question organization also lets improvement change how alternatives are produced. Preference and trajectory thus give recursion material; Yinyang's role return enables both participants to contribute; the Trinity gives improvement a form in life.

### How version history supports recursion

[Writing one's own recursive improvement through literature](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/literary-recursive-improvement.html) connects version operations to this return:

| Version operation | Contribution to recursive inquiry |
| --- | --- |
| Save a commit | Retain a particular description or implementation as a definite object for later comparison. |
| Inspect a diff | Show which relation, method, or code changed. |
| Develop a branch | Give a worthwhile alternative a place for continued construction. |
| Merge and reconstruct | Organize selected contributions into the next arrangement actually used. |
| Return to an older version | Retrieve earlier material under new conditions and make a new selection. |

A recursive loop describes functional return to itself; a directed acyclic commit history records each return happening later. Versions connected to purposes, reasons, and consequences make improvement intelligible, comparable, and continuable. External model progress and local method development jointly change support conditions. Stability, completion, and letting go can also be worthwhile results within this personal practice.

## YY-17 · Personal recursive self-improvement in economic reality

**Invest finite cultivation in embodied, multimodal reasoning in real situations, use available model capabilities as tools, and reselect work allocation as technology and society change.** Personal RSI thereby improves capabilities and the methods for judging which capabilities deserve cultivation.

Time, attention, bodily energy, and money support combinations of cultivation,
model assistance, recovery, and sustaining conditions. Their units and timescales
differ; actual life gains, developing capacities, and total costs inform the
next allocation. Neuroeconomic research examines valuation and control,
psychological inquiry examines effort and competing uses, and socioeconomic
inquiry examines demand, institutions, and the distribution of costs. Together
they help explain a situated choice and revise how worthwhile investment is judged.

[The developed argument](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/economic-personal-rsi.html) and the
[psychological](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/psychological-readings.html#valuation-and-the-allocation-of-effort)
and [engineering](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/engineering-readings.html#physical-conditions-measurement-and-feedback)
reading routes retain the corresponding sources.

Tasks readily formalized, simulated, and checked at low cost more easily enter scaled training and replication. Economic substitution also depends on reliability, demand, and deployment costs; complementarity can increase the value of another human contribution. A person retains foundations needed to understand, detect consequential errors, and connect results to reality while borrowing capabilities already reliably available.

Social models, predictions, and rules change participants and subsequent data. Physiological, psychological, cultural, and institutional interactions require understanding to return continually to activity. Local formalization supplies testable explanations, while feedback following adoption can change their conditions. Embodied participation connects perception, movement, affect, memory, and language within action, bringing situated observation and social experience into calibration.

Low human metabolic power coexists with rich multimodal activity, offering grounds for research and cultivation. Brain power consumption, machine computational capacity, and full-brain simulation costs require comparisons appropriate to their respective objects. Machine embodiment is also developing; the scope of near-term substitution depends on actual reliability and expense. The economic value of a hard-to-automate capability still depends on demand and accessible opportunities.

Shared practice compares personal practice, model support, supervision, maintenance, and opportunity costs, letting observation change candidates and model elaboration enrich subsequent attempts. New capabilities, demand, and failures revise how this allocation is modeled, simulated, evaluated, and modified. [Personal recursive self-improvement in economic reality](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/economic-personal-rsi.html) develops this direction, its argument, and sources. Gains in life also include understanding, expression, relationships, and freely available time.
