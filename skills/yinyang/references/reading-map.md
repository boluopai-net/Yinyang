# Read by the work being done

Start with your question. [YY-00](docs/core-views.md#yy-00) introduces how the
author's four adopted through-lines hold together under P-AR; it is the connected
entrance, not a required route through every essay. Readers outside literature,
history, or philosophy can
select relevant [Core views](docs/core-views.md), examine their grounds and conditions,
and follow the linked essays when a fuller argument would help. Selected ideas
can guide the configuration of a fitting personal environment. The [use statement](https://github.com/boluopai-net/Yinyang/blob/main/README.md#intended-use-and-mental-health)
keeps this distinct from psychological self-help work. Writing your own philosophy
or articles is a further choice of interest, developed in
[An example and a commons](docs/example-and-commons.md). Reading, selective use,
and full adoption are available choices, not compulsory stages.

[Forming and revising working models](design-and-revision.md) connects the six
formation lenses with [native Hindsight formation](hindsight.md). The private
binding index locates complete actual accounts for context supply or retrieval; output
count follows the questions. Original evidence stays with its owners, while
derived Personal understanding can migrate to observations/pages. The
[entrypoint](https://github.com/boluopai-net/Yinyang/blob/main/skills/yinyang-empty/SKILL.md) owns ordinary reading and the
[environment guide](formation/environment.md) connects understanding to use.
Selective borrowing need not configure the entire adopted arrangement.

## Practical materials and supplied context

In a formed personal version, the host may already supply the entrypoint,
adopted Core views and six actual working accounts as complete context. Use
those bodies directly and retrieve only missing content through that version's
declared route. This map locates further material; the model selects and
retrieves what the current work needs.

## Find the contribution the work needs

For how philosophy and the personal environment revise each other, and how their usable results
return to meta-level methods, begin with the
[practice overview](docs/creation-method.md#philosophy-and-the-personal-environment-revise-one-another).
Whether starting from philosophy or an actual environment, use the questions below to locate the
contribution currently missing.

A request does not always disclose the kind of help that will serve it. The
same wish may call for a finished work, an explanation, an objection, or a
shared attempt. The following routes help turn that difference into a usable
arrangement, without making the person first design all of its implementation.

For example, an unfinished spoken objection to an article may need the model
to retrieve the passage, develop a comparison, and maintain the resulting
argument. If the person instead wants to practice composing that argument,
the same material may support an editable beginning and criticism of another
attempt. The difference lies in the purpose and participation being sought;
the available model and tools determine which arrangement can actually work.

## Locate a missing distinction

Sometimes the obstacle is an account that merges things with different
consequences. These readings develop distinctions that help locate the actual
problem before changing the person, the description, or the software.

| What is getting conflated? | Why this resource, and what it makes possible |
| --- | --- |
| A meaningful image, a representation, and an effective realization | [World, process, and expression](docs/world-and-expression.md) relates the ongoing world, a question-selected computational model, its expression, and its realization. It helps ask whether the missing relation is in the model, the way it is made available, or the arrangement acting on it; its bounded computational example shows what representability and simulation require. |
| Remembering an unfinished action, completing it, feeling relief, and developing a capacity | [Shared reflection and continued cultivation](docs/participation-and-support.md#continuation-records-and-relief) explains how these results can connect without being interchangeable. Use it to determine whether the remaining need is a retrievable account, carried action, a change in experienced burden, or later participation showing growth. |
| A conflict about the same thing and descriptions of different times or conditions | [Forming a life trajectory](docs/flow-and-time.md) distinguishes occurrence, reference, experience, and update rates. It helps reconstruct what each statement concerns; a temporal distinction can clarify disagreement without automatically dissolving a genuine contradiction. |
| What exists, what is warranted, what is wanted, and how to proceed | [Personal-First Humanism](docs/personal-first-humanism.md) develops the author's value orientation alongside ontology, epistemology, and plural methods. It helps show where a purpose selects a direction and where a factual claim still needs independent warrant, including whose costs enter a proposed improvement. |
| A successful output, a better collaboration, and human development | [Yinyang: core views](docs/core-views.md), especially [YY-01](docs/core-views.md#yy-01), [YY-07](docs/core-views.md#yy-07), and [YY-14](docs/core-views.md#yy-14), connect the claims to their developed arguments. Use the stable IDs to identify the claim at issue, then follow the essay when its reasons or conditions matter. |

## Investigate a disputed interpretation or inference

The useful next reading depends on what remains uncertain. A missing candidate,
an unsupported inference, and an impractical abstraction are different problems;
they do not all call for more data or stronger confidence.

| Question arising in inquiry | Reading and its contribution |
| --- | --- |
| Which possibilities are supported, what holds across readings, and when does preference shape them? | The [inconsistency-tolerant semantics appendix](docs/inconsistency-tolerant-semantics.md) owns the formal repair families and answer conditions. [YY-10](docs/core-views.md#yy-10) and [selection and inference](docs/preference-harness.md#selection-and-inference) develop their use as natural-language judgments: Brave retains a supported possibility, AR checks the same conclusion across relevant readings, and IAR proceeds from common grounds. An applicable preference can select readings before the answer test; separately supported claims need joint support before being asserted together. |
| How does shared work leave a life trajectory open to exploration and reflection? | [Forming a life trajectory](docs/flow-and-time.md) uses a jointly improved reading environment to gather histories through two main entrances, Git/GitHub version history and ChatGPT’s Computer History. It distinguishes event sources from current accounts and develops how retention, time, and revision support continuation. |
| How does a question emerge, and what would distinguish competing explanations? | [Reasoning within an unfinished stream](docs/reasoning.md) develops event streams, question-relative views, abduction, prediction, retained sources, candidates, and nested feedback. It helps generate a candidate and derive a consequence that further interaction can actually discriminate. |
| How can conflicting materials remain available while a coherent view supports action? | [How preference participates in choice and reasoning](docs/preference-harness.md) distinguishes retaining sources, selecting views, and inferring within them. It helps keep a rejected current candidate available for reconsideration without treating incompatible assertions as jointly accepted. Its psychological and formal sources have different roles. |
| How can a selection pattern become available for reconsideration? | [Making an implicit selection pattern visible](docs/preference-harness.md#making-an-implicit-selection-pattern-visible) connects adopted arrangements and retained histories with an inspectable, revisable account of preference. | Is a richer model useful and feasible for this question? | [Finite inquiry and economical abstraction](docs/finite-inquiry-and-abstraction.md) develops limited evidence, selective retention, and the cost of abstraction. | How should an account learn from practical participation or make a research claim? | [Embodied Experimental Philosophy](docs/embodied-experimental-philosophy.md) distinguishes practical and research loops, public works, and revisable boundaries and aims. It helps recognize what a situated success has shown and what broader explanatory claim still needs inquiry at its own scale. |
| How does written philosophy change understanding through responses and opposition? | [Speech, opposition, and self-formation](docs/speech-opposition-and-self-formation.md) is the formation part's second essay: it moves from speaking and retroaction to reconstructing premises and relationships, preparing content for personal models. ## Understand the author's connected philosophy

[Writing recursive self-improvement through literature](docs/literary-recursive-improvement.md) is a separate technical chapter: it connects body, generative description, and interpreter through RSI research, explaining the practical benefit of condensation and how improvement methods themselves enter revision.

[Personal recursive self-improvement in economic reality](docs/economic-personal-rsi.md) then considers finite learning investment and comparative advantage: cultivate embodied, multimodal, situated judgment, use model capabilities as tools, and revise work allocation with economic and social conditions.

The collection also includes [Why write a personal philosophy](docs/why-yinyang.md),
the [Argument for Cuteness](docs/meng.md), and
[Baby Heraclitus](docs/baby-heraclitus.md), which connect shared direction,
conditions for learning, and the person being cultivated.
[Literary programming](docs/literary-programming.md) develops how to write that
philosophy; [Machines and becoming](docs/deleuze-and-becoming.md) explores a
focused comparison with Deleuze and Guattari from an established practice.

These essays explain why the author organizes the practice as Yinyang. They
can be read for their arguments and literary form, whether or not the reader
intends to use the Skill. A reader already working on a concrete problem can
enter the relevant argument directly; the Sun–Moon–Earth image is not a
theoretical prerequisite for using the methods above.

| Question about the example | Reading and the understanding it develops |
| --- | --- |
| Why borrow generative capability, and why choose cultivation as its use? | [Borrowing fire and cultivating creative capacity](docs/stealing-fire.md) connects collective acquisition, finite life, philosophical clarification, and cultivation. It explains how understanding, desire, capacities, and surrounding conditions can change together, and why available power leaves purposes open to judgment. |
| What work do Sun, Moon, Earth, and Taiji do in this philosophy? | [Mutual becoming through Yinyang and Taiji](docs/yinyang-and-taiji.md) develops ceaseless transformation, mutual grounding, and the Shuang yu. It makes differentiated generation, reception, shaping, and worldly consequences intelligible as relations; the actual participants remain able to exceed or exchange the pictured roles. |
| How can personal material and models of both participants become a usable practical arrangement? | [From personal models to a sinthome](docs/writing-a-sinthome.md) specifies the concrete content of what this account calls a sinthome, develops its formation, representation, realization, and use, and explains the term's Lacanian source. |
| How do philosophy, psychology, and engineering belong to one practice? | [The trinity of philosophy, psychology, and engineering](docs/local-source-and-website.md) connects one changing physical reality, jointly developed computational models, and differentiated contributions with local, repository, and website forms. It helps understand how a philosophical account can gain a working realization and a public expression without those forms becoming identical. |

For a connected conceptual reading, purpose → core views → practice explanation
offers one possible route. The arrows indicate an intelligible connection,
not a syllabus to finish before useful work or a compulsory adoption sequence.

## Return to sources when the claim needs them

When terms such as the Other, the divided subject, retroaction, the three registers, or the sinthome need clarification, [A guide to Lacanian concepts](docs/lacan-reading-guide.md) offers an introduction and review outside the main sequence: begin with their conceptual relations, then return to the relevant argument and original text.

The source guides help investigate a particular premise; they are not another
general reading requirement. [Philosophical readings](docs/philosophical-readings.md)
locates Heraclitus, Yinyang/Taiji, Hegel, Wittgenstein, the Buddhist raft passage,
Freud, and Lacan, including the limits
of the author's interpretations. [Psychological readings](docs/psychological-readings.md)
connects process, evidence, psychological theories, belief, reasoning, and
interaction when an account makes a psychological claim.
[Engineering readings](docs/engineering-readings.md) supplies paths into self-study,
command lines, Git, software design, algorithms, experiments, and physical
conditions when an intended realization needs those capabilities. A source's
presence in a guide does not establish that it supports every proposed use.

These links resolve within the complete Yinyang directory. The

identify missing material rather than claiming to have read it. Private
provenance stays at `references/personal/sources.md` in a formed private package;
the public [evidence guide](formation/revision.md#sources-and-evidence) explains its maintenance.
