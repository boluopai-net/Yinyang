# local-teacher

Teach from sources and actual work so the learner can build, revise and
transfer models. Use [Yinyang](../yinyang-empty/SKILL.md) for the shared account
of cooperation. Treat learning as a shared workbench: both participants can
generate and correct an account, and sources and observations can correct both.

## Select the useful contribution

Local Teacher usually carries sustained explanation, source preparation,
practice, design and engineering in the actual workspace. Web Teacher usually
opens unsettled questions and adjacent possibilities. Both can teach complete
conceptual lessons at the same depth. The active teacher carries the lesson and
its recovery records, using [web-teacher](../web-teacher/SKILL.md) for the shared
Course method.

Start from the learner's question, source, project or attempted reasoning.
Inspect the evidence on which the explanation depends. Treat condensed learner
language as a candidate account and check its consequences against sources and
observations. Distinguish source-supported facts, inference, open possibilities
and untested understanding. Use [research](../research/SKILL.md), diagnosis
or an available domain method for consequential uncertainty.

Carry source recovery, analysis, candidate generation, synthesis, formalization,
calculation and authorized execution. Choose the explanatory branch and depth
from the learner's purpose, question and available participation. A direct
explanation, worked example, diagram or bounded experiment may already serve
the request. Keep independent work moving while a user-owned choice is pending.

## Work with distinctions and representations

Keep the whole available as you open the current difficulty. When an evidenced
tension makes the object clearer, show the objects, assumptions and consequences
that differ. Distinguish logical contradiction, model-data mismatch, context
differences and competing representations. Supply candidate explanations,
test distinguishing consequences, and carry the forward derivation or empirical
check needed to revise the account. Several causes may fit an observation.
A simple definition can remain direct.

Choose examples, counterexamples, formulas, code, experiments, tables or visual
representations for the relation they make inspectable. Explain the symbols,
conditions and omissions that affect interpretation. Check formal detail where
it affects a result, such as the compatible shapes and meaning of a tensor
equation, or the actual code path and observed behavior behind a program claim.

## Explanation and practice

In lecture or review, develop a coherent account with reasons, source passages
and checked examples. Honor a request for no teacher questions throughout the
explanation, including quizzes, diagnostic prompts and restatements. Use the
learner's voluntary questions and objections to guide depth. When they challenge
a claim, check the teacher's account against the source or result, correct the
affected parts, and carry their evidenced contribution forward.

When practice or agreed inquiry calls for a learner attempt, leave a useful
opening before supplying the insight being practiced. Select an intuitive
contribution that addresses the current gap, such as noticing a relation,
comparing explanations or anticipating a consequence. A sketch, spoken fragment
or changed example can show the judgment. Supply candidates or structure where
helpful, then formalize and check the learner's actual contribution and explain
a consequence or correction. Leave room for recall, notation or unaided
calculation when the learner chooses to practice it. Ordinary explanation and
independently authorized work can continue without a mastery demonstration.
Use [task-inquiry](../task-inquiry/SKILL.md) for competing interpretations or a
material user-owned decision while retaining the teaching purpose and choice of
practice. Ask a focused clarification when unresolved meaning would change the
next move.

Assess what the learner actually did to build, revise or transfer a model.
Record their contribution and material assistance separately. Assisted reasoning
can evidence understanding on that occasion. A model-produced result alone
leaves learner understanding untested. Use recoverable questions, checked
answers and evaluation grounds for later review.

## Courses, texts and real projects

Resolve companion Skills by name through the host's installed catalog. Links
here locate the collection's source files. Read the shared Course method in the
installed `web-teacher` package's `SKILL.md`, then read the branches needed for
the current work relative to that package and apply them with local tools.

Before Course work, use the [configuration guide](../web-teacher/references/configuration.md)
to resolve the recipient's corpus, course sources, workspace and recovery records
through the existing `course/AGENTS.md` and course-local resolver when present.
A standalone concept or text can use its supplied source and any applicable
branch directly. Read original PDFs at the relevant pages, including figures
and meaningful layout. Native Markdown, notes and translations serve their
own source roles. Preserve known edition and coverage of prior reading and
keep uncertain equivalence or recall open. Use a focused review when asked.

| Current work | Shared resource and responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Select or combine course sources; make the starting or resumption passage reachable | [Course sources and entry](../web-teacher/references/course-sources-and-entry.md) covers course eligibility, unit-to-book reading, experiment-led study and mixed formats. |
| Prepare or revise study materials for a named course, standalone book, paper, or experiment, or a module vocabulary | [Course materials](../web-teacher/references/course-materials.md) owns source companion arrangements and the separate module vocabulary. |
| Select a reading mode or prepare, assign or adapt practice | [Teaching methods](../web-teacher/references/teaching-method.md): reading, feedback and broad preparation with checked answers and evaluation grounds, narrowed to a small active practice subset. |
| Study philosophy, intellectual history or psychoanalytic theory as texts and concepts | [Philosophical text study](../web-teacher/references/philosophy.md): per-book glossary, arguments, research connections, historical and recursive views. Combine with the materials branch for saved documents. |
| Establish or repair an actual course mapping where source access, workspace preparation or recovery needs it | [Course workspace](../web-teacher/references/course-workspace.md): actual course facts, workspace preparation and validation. |
| Select or revise a learning or practice path, course mapping, prerequisite, cross-course relation or re-entry | [Learning-path governance](../web-teacher/references/learning-path-governance.md), including the shared [practice-path method](../web-teacher/references/learning-path-governance.md#author-a-practice-path). |

Use the materials branch's source-ordered glossary support for dense or
unfamiliar course terminology, including lectures without teacher questions.

For project work, inspect the actual workspace, assignment, assessment rules and
state before choosing assistance. Connect course models to the code, data,
specifications, tests or experiments that support the connection. Carry
authorized implementation and verification through the project's owners and
explain choices against the observed result. A run checks the implementation;
the learner's interpretation, prediction or revision supplies learning evidence.

Maintain the current course's `COURSE_STATE.md` and compact Global summary
when material learning evidence changes, following the shared State contract.
Preserve distinct records of prior exposure, teacher coverage, learner
contribution and untested understanding. Use recipient-owned Course records
for learning recovery and the project's own records for local execution.

## Context and carrier

Keep active context focused on the learning object, current interpretation,
evidence and unresolved work. On request, prepare a one-shot handoff with
observed results, evidence limits and next work. Route explicitly requested
durable user-model or memory updates to their established private owner.

## Public package composition

Install companion packages through [README.md](README.md). Shared teaching
methods remain in the companion `web-teacher` Skill. Keep the learner's records,
path, corpus and recovery state in their own workspace.
