# Writing the Revision Account

[简体中文](revision.zh-CN.md) · [Writing the six accounts](overview.md) · [Shared writing principles](shared.md) · [Practical revision method](revision-method.md)

The revision account answers how the people and models in this particular
practice reconsider what they understand, what they do, and how they change
either. Describe what can open a revision, whose contribution can change the
judgment, and how the partners know what the change accomplished.

## Use a revision you can recover

Start from a consequential example: a person corrects an interpretation,
evidence challenges a premise, or an arrangement has an unexpected effect.
Read the original expression, the interpretation previously adopted, the new
grounds, and the observable result where available. Name what each source
supports. A successful adaptation may show better access, while the proposed
explanation of a person's experience remains open.

Describe the triggering event and the relation it reopened. Show how the
participants determined whether to revise a local statement, a division of
work, an environmental realization, or the original aim. State the conditions
under which evidence from a second activity would be needed. Where the case
remains open, identify the further experience, judgment, or result that could
change what follows.

## Show how the correction travels

Trace one significant change from its grounds to its observed consequence.
Explain which of the other accounts it affects and where their evidence belongs.
Give each dependency a reason. For instance, a change in what the person wants
to learn can alter both the counterpart's expected contribution and the place
where it appears in the working environment. Show what the participants can
inspect once the change reaches use.

The environment account describes the current route and its effects. The
revision account explains how use can alter that route and the understanding
that motivated it. A runbook may supply commands or routes for carrying out
the change. Here, name the maintained route where readers need it and preserve
the purposes, grounds, choices, and limits that give the procedure its meaning.

## Let experience revise the method itself

Use a passage such as this fictional example to make the return concrete:

> We used to mark a reading aid complete when its notes were saved. During
> a real return to the text, the reader could find the notes but could not
> reach the exact paragraph they discussed. We changed the acceptance check
> to start at the reader's ordinary entry and follow one note back to its
> passage. The next session verified that route. Whether the aid also improves
> the reader's independent interpretation remains a separate question.

The example identifies the earlier judgment, the observation that changed it,
and the revised way of checking the work. In your account, explain what belongs
to the person to decide, what the model can investigate or implement, and how
either participant can reopen a provisional choice. An adopted method may
change from one setting to another as participants, available sources, and
ways of working change.

End with a check the partners can actually use. Follow dependencies across
the six accounts, keep consequential uncertainty with the affected claim, and
show what was available to the intended reader or user. The [practical revision
method](revision-method.md) develops ways to carry out that work.
